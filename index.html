WordNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

WordNest ("the app") is a word puzzle game. This policy explains what information is used when you play.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Your game progress (levels, coins, streak and words learned) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Internet access

The app uses the internet to load ads and fonts. The game itself does not send your data to any server we operate.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
